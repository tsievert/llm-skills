# Role scope: postdoc and tenure track

The skill is for **postdoctoral** and **tenure-track / faculty** applications. Doctoral (PhD student, doctoral
candidate, graduate programme) positions are out of scope: say so and stop, and when researching for this skill,
discard advice written for them.

## Names to recognise (and to search with)

| Level | English names | Finnish names (for local-language checks) |
|---|---|---|
| Postdoc | postdoctoral researcher, postdoctoral fellow, research fellow, Academy Research Fellow (early independent) | tutkijatohtori, Akatemiatutkija |
| Tenure track, entry | assistant professor, tenure-track assistant professor, junior faculty, tenure track level 2 | apulaisprofessori, nuorempi apulaisprofessori, tenure track -tutkija |
| Tenure track, next | associate professor, tenure track level 3 | apulaisprofessori (associate level) |
| Faculty, open level | "Tenure track position (Assistant, Associate or Professor)" | tenure track -professuuri |

Translations of Finnish titles vary; use the title the employer uses (TENK rule). Many ads cover several levels:
check which level the cover letter must name (some calls require that statement).

## What differs by role (observations, check against the call)

| Document | Postdoc | Tenure track |
|---|---|---|
| Cover letter | About one page; fit with the lab or project, relevant skills, ability to finish work; for unsolicited applications a proposed direction | One to two pages; short preview of the research programme, funding record, fit with the department and named colleagues, link to teaching |
| Research statement or plan | Past work plus a project that fits the time frame; a feasible plan | Largest weight on the future; independent of the postdoc supervisor; fundable over several years; readable for a mixed panel |
| Teaching | Often minor, unless the call asks | Teaching portfolio or statement usually required and weighed; say what the person can teach |
| Other | Reference letters, sample papers | Highlighted publications, funding record, service, societal interaction, leadership |

Sources and caveats: `research/application-style-coach-ledger.md` section 12. Most advice found is US, field-specific,
blog or commercial level; treat it as a prompt for questions, never as a rule. The call and the TENK integrity rule
override any advice to "hype" results.

## Assistant vs associate level (Finnish calls)

Many calls are open to both levels and judge the level during recruitment. Assistant level: potential, independence,
early funding activity. Associate level: already significant merits, funding and leadership evidence. Some calls ask
the letter to name the level for evaluation; the CV and plan must then support it. Postdoc calls in Finland are lighter
(a one to two page letter, a TENK CV, an Academy-format list, sometimes a short research description). Junior group
leader calls at European institutes are an adjacent role: short research proposal, independence and institute fit.
Details and sources: ledger section 12.5.
