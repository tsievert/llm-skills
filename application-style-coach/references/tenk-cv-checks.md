# TENK researcher's CV: formatting and phrasing checks

Source: TENK "Researcher's Curriculum Vitae Template" (recommendation 2020; current files on
https://tenk.fi/en/advice-and-materials/template-researchers-curriculum-vitae, docx revised 10 April 2026).
Headings below were checked against the 2025-12 and 2026-04 docx files (they differ by one added notice about
the Research.fi CV download) and the 2020 PDF. Re-check the TENK page if the user says the template changed.

## Status of the template

- A recommendation, not a law. TENK asks organisations to adopt the headings and structure in original form and to
  give specific instructions for each situation. The writer follows the instructions of whoever requested the CV.
- Purpose: merits shown "comprehensively, truthfully and comparably". Everything must be verifiable; a CV in an
  application is in principle a public document. Suspected exaggeration or distortion of merits (also in
  translations, and also in research plans, statements and portfolios) can be treated as a suspected violation
  of responsible conduct of research.
- Headings 1-15 matter most; the bullets under them are illustrative.
- A CV in this template can be generated from a Research.fi profile (from April 2026). Mention it only if the
  user asks how to start; the assistant does not use the profile.

## The 15 headings (use these titles verbatim)

| # | Heading | What belongs there (short) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Personal details and the date of the CV | Surname incl. previous surnames, first names, researcher ID (ORCID), date of the CV |
| 2 | Degrees | Most recent first: date of certificate, title, major, institution, locality, country; contact of the organisation that granted the highest degree; Title of Docent |
| 3 | Other education and expertise | Date, name, scope and provider of other training |
| 4 | Language skills | Native language; level and certificate date or justified self-assessment |
| 5 | Current employment | Start/end, title, employer, place; part-time stated; career stage I-IV; secondary occupations |
| 6 | Previous work experience | Most recent first, incl. grant periods and long visits abroad |
| 7 | Career breaks | Optional; dates and duration in months |
| 8 | Research funding and grants | Dates, type, source, amount, own role, name of the PI |
| 9 | Research output | Total publications and e.g. ten most important and/or most cited (name the database); OA links; list as separate appendix by Ministry classes; methods, software, infrastructures, data, materials, guides, tools; patents; artistic works |
| 10 | Research supervision and leadership experience | Numbers of supervisees by programme (no names), principal or co-supervisor; leadership experience |
| 11 | Teaching merits | Carefully selected; pedagogical training; development of teaching; experience; teaching funding |
| 12 | Awards and honours | Includes recognition of teaching |
| 13 | Other key academic merits | Examiner/opponent roles, reviewing, editorial roles, committees, invited lectures, conference organising |
| 14 | Scientific and societal impact | Open science, use of outputs, research integrity work, communication, media |
| 15 | Other merits | Company or organisation work, other expertise relevant to the purpose of the CV |

## Formatting and phrasing checks

Raise these as observations or questions; the user fixes them.

1. **Order and titles.** Are the 15 headings present in order with TENK's wording, and are empty ones simply left out
   rather than renamed? (Many calls allow leaving out headings that do not apply; see "When a call differs".)
2. **Reverse chronology** in headings 2, 5, 6, 8; consistent date format (one style only; the script flags mixes).
3. **Official names.** Institutions, degrees and job titles as the employer or funder uses them. Translated titles must
   be the official or the organisation's own translation or otherwise justifiable; no self-made translations.
4. **Facts instead of adjectives.** Counts, amounts, durations, roles. "Extensive teaching experience" becomes a
   number of courses, levels and years (the user supplies them).
5. **Own role.** Headings 8 and 10: is the user's role stated (applicant, co-applicant, PI named)? Supervision:
   principal or co-supervisor, number of supervisees by programme, no names.
6. **Part-time and breaks.** Part-time work stated; breaks in months if included.
7. **Heading 9.** Database named if citation counts are given; publication list as a separate appendix; publications
   marked published, accepted, submitted or planned with the same words throughout (no unaccepted manuscripts in a
   Ministry-classified list). Check whether the call forbids metrics (see below).
8. **Heading 11.** "Carefully selected": is the selection a choice for this position, or everything ever taught?
9. **No unexplained abbreviations; no photo** unless the call wants one (RCF and many Finnish calls say no images).
10. **Date of the CV** present (heading 1) and the CV only contains merits up to the call deadline when the call says so.
11. **Consistency with the cover letter** and the publication list (same titles, dates, spelling).

## When a call differs from TENK

- Calls often say "should include the sections of the TENK template, need not follow the format", or name no format
  at all. Then TENK is the default for a CV, the call's limits and requested items win, and the assistant says so
  rather than insisting on all 15 headings.
- The Research Council of Finland (Academy of Finland) CV template is derived from TENK but shorter: it asks for the
  headings needed for the call type, a page limit (three pages in the Fellowship CV guideline checked 2026-10-30),
  no images, justification of role and choice for the up-to-ten key outputs, and no journal rankings, impact
  factors or citation metrics (h-index). That conflicts with TENK heading 9's "most cited publications" wording:
  when a call or funder forbids metrics, the call wins; when silent, using metrics is the user's choice.
- If limits are tight, the assistant may point out that headings 3, 4, 7, 12 and 15 are the usual candidates to shorten
  or omit when the call allows it, but never decides which merits go.

## Decisions that stay with the user

- Career breaks (heading 7): optional per TENK and "may have a positive impact"; one university ad asks for clear
  reporting, another ad says not to include family circumstances. Say what each call says; do not choose.
- Metrics in heading 9 when the call is silent.
- Which ten publications or outputs are "most important", and the wording of the justification.
- Whether to include sensitive personal data (health, family): warn that the CV may be public and that some calls
  forbid it; the decision is theirs.
